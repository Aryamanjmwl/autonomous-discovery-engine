# Research and IP Notes

ADE is a private research prototype. Keep the repository private unless there is an explicit decision to disclose or publish parts of the work.

## Original Work

Document original ideas, experiments, architectural decisions, and review notes in `docs/invention_log.md`, `docs/experiment_log.md`, and `docs/technical_decisions.md`.

Separate normal open-source dependencies from original ADE logic. Using libraries such as NumPy, Pillow, PyYAML, pandas, pytest, OpenCV, or scikit-learn as dependencies is different from copying implementation code from another project.

## Public Disclosure

Avoid public disclosure of proprietary methods, private experiments, unpublished claims, or sensitive datasets before review.

Do not describe candidate findings as validated discoveries. Use careful language such as candidate anomaly, candidate pattern, candidate concept, possible relationship, hypothesis, and requires human review.

## Patent and Legal Caution

These notes are not legal advice and do not make any claim of patentability. Before filing, publishing, fundraising with technical claims, or disclosing proprietary methods, seek professional IP/legal review.
